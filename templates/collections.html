Collections whitelist

Story collections, anthologies and omnibuses are treated like paperbacks: left out unless their title is on this whitelist. Illustrated and fine-press editions are kept anyway. A book is recognised as a collection by its title ("stories", "novels", "anthology"...) or by the book notes Claude writes for each candidate.

Keep puts a title on the whitelist; Leave out records that it shouldn't be; unanswered titles are left out too. Answers apply to the next run.
