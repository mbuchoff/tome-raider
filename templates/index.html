How to use it
  • Stand square to the shelf and take the photo at full resolution, about 15–30 spines across. Several photos of the same section can go in one scan.
  • Pick the shelf category first: it sets how strict the rules are and the minimum value ($50 on thrift and cheap shelves, $100 on rare ones). Extremely rare and signed shelves have no minimum: they list every book with a sign of value in check order, likeliest value first, and look for signed stickers. If one photo mixes shelves, say so below, counting shelves from the top.
  • A pull means "worth a look", not "valuable". The Check line on each card says what to confirm in hand; the spine crop is the surest way to find it.
  • Please mark each pull Worth it or Not worth it, and mark Should pull on any book in the "not pulled" list that was a find. That's how the rules get tuned.
  • After about 20 idle minutes the app sleeps, and the first page takes 30–60 seconds to wake it.
